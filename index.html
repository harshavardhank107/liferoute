The Emergency Routing Crisis

Traditional dispatch algorithms rely solely on GPS proximity, causing preventable deaths.

🏥

Nearest is not best

Taking severe stroke or STEMI cardiac patients to the closest clinic without a Cath Lab or CT scanner causes fatal secondary transfers (+45 min delay).

🛏️

Beds change while you drive

ER beds fluctuate constantly. During transit, walk-in admissions can saturate capacity, creating ambulance offload ramping delays outside emergency bays.

💥

Mass-casualty events overwhelm one ER

Disasters funnel 80% of casualties to the single closest hospital, causing acute collapse while nearby tertiary trauma facilities sit under-utilized.
